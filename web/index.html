SEC reportorial deadline tracker for Philippine corporations

The General Information Sheet is due thirty calendar days after your own annual meeting, so no two corporations share that date. The audited financial statements are due on the schedule the Commission publishes for the season. This keeps both per company, with escalating reminders and a record of what was filed and the reference it came back with.

Non-working days

A submission made on a Saturday, Sunday or holiday counts as filed on the next working day, so a deadline landing on one is not a grace day. Philippine holidays are proclaimed yearly and this tool never goes online, so add the ones that matter from the proclamation.
